🪟 Windows Requirements

Windows 11 Specs

CPU: 1 GHz or faster, 2+ cores, 64-bit (Intel 8th Gen / AMD Ryzen 2000+)
RAM: 4 GB minimum
Storage: 64 GB available storage
Firmware: UEFI & Secure Boot capable
Security: TPM version 2.0 chip enabled
GPU: DirectX 12 compatible with WDDM 2.0

Windows 10 Specs

CPU: 1 GHz or faster compatible processor or SoC
RAM: 1 GB for 32-bit or 2 GB for 64-bit
Storage: 16 GB for 32-bit or 20 GB for 64-bit OS
GPU: DirectX 9 or later with WDDM 1.0 driver
Display: 800 x 600 minimum resolution

Windows 8 / 8.1 Specs

CPU: 1 GHz with support for PAE, NX, and SSE2 features
RAM: 1 GB (32-bit) or 2 GB (64-bit)
Storage: 16 GB (32-bit) or 20 GB (64-bit) disk space
GPU: Microsoft DirectX 9 graphics device with WDDM driver

Windows 7 Specs

CPU: 1 GHz or faster 32-bit (x86) or 64-bit (x64)
RAM: 1 GB (32-bit) or 2 GB (64-bit)
Storage: 16 GB (32-bit) or 20 GB (64-bit) standard HDD space
GPU: DirectX 9 graphics with WDDM 1.0 or higher driver
